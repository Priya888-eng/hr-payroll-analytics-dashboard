# HR & Payroll Analytics — Business Insights

1. **Production department has both the lowest average salary and lowest average tenure** — suggests pay may be driving early attrition in this department.

2. **Turnover rate is 36.7%** — voluntarily-terminated employees showed *higher* engagement scores than those who stayed, and "Another position" is the top termination reason. This points to losing engaged talent to better-paying opportunities elsewhere, not a culture/morale problem.

3. **Termination reasons vary by department**, meaning HR should apply different retention levers per department rather than a one-size-fits-all policy.
